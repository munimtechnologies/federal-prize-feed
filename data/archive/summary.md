## Cross-check

Georgetown CSET counted 607 federal prize competitions on Challenge.gov for 2020-2025. This archive has 186 challenges whose submission window closed in those years. Its last deadline is 2021-11-19, so it does not cover the later years; GSA's 2026 toolkit puts the all-time total near 1,300 challenges.

## Limits

- The archive holds only what agencies posted on Challenge.gov, and it stops at the date above. Some federal prizes were never listed there.
- Cash is the amount offered, as the agency wrote it, not what was paid out. When the purse field is empty, the single prizes are added up (`prize list`; a phase listed twice would be counted twice), and failing that the largest dollar amount in the tagline is used (`tagline`).
- Submission, participant, winner and awarded-cash counts are filled in for few challenges, mostly from the older platform, so the participation figures describe that subset only.
- Departments come from the `agency` field or, in older files, `groupName`, with common spellings and abbreviations merged; agencies.csv lists every original spelling next to its department.
- Years follow the submission end date (or the start date when there is no end date).
- Names and emails of challenge managers are not copied.
